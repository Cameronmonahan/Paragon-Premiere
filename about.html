Our story

Why we started Paragon Premiere

After 20+ years building custom homes across the Wasatch Front, Brandon Jensen kept hearing the same thing from homeowners: it's easy to find someone to build your dream home, but nearly impossible to find someone you trust to take care of it afterward.

Paragon Premiere was built to close that gap — with a membership model built around one idea: your fee pays for professional oversight and expertise, not marked-up filters and light bulbs. We built one master maintenance protocol, and your tier simply determines how often we're in your home.

Every recurring visit, repair, and coordinated project runs through one trusted team, so you never have to explain your home's history to someone new.
