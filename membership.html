Billing philosophy

White-glove, without the hidden markup

We don't charge $15 labor to add a bag of water softener salt. Routine service labor performed during a scheduled visit is included in your membership. Consumables, materials, repairs, and specialty or third-party work are billed separately — clearly, at cost plus a transparent management fee.

Included in your visit

  • Inspect & replace furnace filter
  • Inspect & refill water softener salt
  • Inspect & replace refrigerator filter
  • Replace smoke / CO detector batteries
  • Clean dishwasher filter
  • Lubricate garage door
  • Clean ice maker when scheduled

Billed separately, itemized

  • Furnace & refrigerator filters
  • RO cartridges
  • Water softener salt
  • Batteries & replacement smoke detectors
  • Humidifier pad, UV bulb
  • Parts & materials
  • Repairs, specialty & third-party services

Seasonal Care & Complete Care

Visits change with the seasons, not just the calendar

Pricing doesn't change by season — the checklist does. In Utah, that means recovering from winter, preparing for it, and everything in between.

Spring

Recover + Activate

Irrigation startup, hose bib inspection, AC startup, exterior drainage, roof & gutter winter damage, window screens, exterior caulking, snow-melt shutdown.

Summer

Performance

AC performance & condenser cleanliness, irrigation, exterior drainage, exterior finishes, pest activity, window & door operation, outdoor kitchen.

Fall

Prepare + Protect

Furnace operation, humidifier, irrigation winterization coordination, hose bibs, gutters, roof, snow-melt activation, weatherstripping, fireplaces.

Winter

Protect + Interior

Furnace & humidification, freeze risks, snow-melt performance, interior plumbing leaks, appliances, smoke/CO, fireplaces, garage equipment.
